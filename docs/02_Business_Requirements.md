# Business Requirements

## Customer
- Login with Phone OTP or Email OTP
- Aadhaar used only for KYC
- View purchased products
- View warranty
- View wallet
- Refer up to 3 customers per eligible product
- Browse categories

## Distributor
- Inventory
- Orders
- Referral tree
- Earnings
- Reports

## Admin
- Manage users
- Products
- Referral policies
- Wallet adjustments
- Reports
