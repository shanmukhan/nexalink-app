# Customer Screens

## Login
Fields:
- Phone or Email
- OTP login (preferred)
- Biometric login
- Remember device

## Home
Sections:
- Wallet summary
- Active order
- Featured products
- Categories
- Referral summary
- Notifications

## Product Details
- Gallery
- Specifications
- Warranty
- Cashback eligibility
- Referral policy
- Similar products

## Cart
- Quantity
- Coupon
- Wallet usage
- Estimated cashback

## Checkout
- Address
- Payment
- Wallet
- GST invoice option

## Orders
Status timeline:
Placed > Paid > Packed > Shipped > Delivered

## Referral Tree
- Expand/collapse tree
- Referral count
- Eligible referrals remaining

## Wallet
- Ledger
- Credits
- Debits
- Withdrawals
- Cashback history

## Notifications
Grouped by:
Orders
Wallet
Referral
Offers

## Profile
Mandatory:
- Name
- Gender
- Aadhaar (encrypted/KYC)

Optional:
- Religious places wishlist
- Product wishlist
- Referral contacts
- Employment references
- Foreign tour wishlist
